## Direction contract

THESIS: Every screen looks and behaves like the store's own paper invoice pad brought to life — not a generic admin panel wearing this store's logo, but literally "your talonario, now electronic and DIAN-legal." Refuses the sterile SaaS-dashboard default (cards, soft shadows, indigo accent) this category always ships.

OWN-WORLD: Warm invoice-paper cream ground, near-black ink text, a violet-gray "carbon-smudge" duplicate-ghost texture behind completed/read-only rows, the logo's signal red reserved for a rubber-stamp mark on accepted/paid/confirmed states and the one active accent — never a wash. Dashed perforated-edge dividers (die-cut rule) separate sections instead of plain hairlines or shadowed cards. A monospace/typewriter face carries amounts, references, and invoice/document numbers (the "carbon typewriter" register); a plain humanist sans carries prose and labels.

STORY: An employee opens Venta and instantly recognizes the shape of the invoice pad they used for years — line items filling in like a hand-totaled form, a red rubber-stamp "ACEPTADO POR LA DIAN" landing the moment Dataico confirms. An admin reviewing the dashboard or caja history feels like flipping through a ledger of stamped talonario copies, not scrolling a generic reports page.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Venta's line-item table — each row sits on a faint hand-ruled horizontal line, the running total set large and underlined twice (the old "total a pagar, subrayado doble" convention), "Facturar"/"Cotizar" styled as stamping/signing the form. Status/paid states show the red rubber-stamp mark.

FORM: Impeccable's Pick card ("Talonario reencarnado"), chosen by the user over the dice-assigned "Diagrama de despiece" (index 4) and the split-flap/cathode-numeral challengers — seed key 62d38ac5.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
